# Code of Conduct

YuktAI is built for every user, and its community should welcome everyone too.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, caste, religion, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, level of experience, education, or where they come from.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Respect different opinions and experience.
- Give and accept helpful feedback about the work, not the person.
- Write so others can understand you; English or any Indian language is fine.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment in public or in private.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or images.
- Spam, trolling, or other behaviour that would be unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/sandeep-miriyala). Every report will be read and handled fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

## What happens

Depending on what happened: a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project.
